# Shutter Manager 1.0 Release Notes

The first release. iPhone and iPad, iOS 18.6 or later. Free, with a tip jar; no account, ads or
analytics.

## Inventory
- Bodies, lenses, lighting, support, filters, drones, audio, power, memory cards, bags and
  accessories, with fields for each kind: mount, sensor format and resolution, focal length and
  aperture, filter thread and strength, guide number, card capacity and speed, and more.
- Photos, serial numbers, notes, purchase price and an optional current value. Marketplace
  searches on used-gear sites.
- Fill in a camera's or lens's details from a photo it took, or scan a product barcode.
- Shutter counts charted against the rated life, with a warning near the end of it. Nikon photos
  give the count straight from the file.
- Sensor-cleaning reminders, a maintenance log, and gear lent out or borrowed with due dates.

## Tools
- Equivalent exposure, ND filters with a bulb timer, and Sunny 16.
- Depth of field and hyperfocal distance, field of view and crop equivalents.
- Astro exposure (NPF and 500 rules), star trails, time-lapse, flash and print size.
- Film reciprocity from the makers' datasheets, and a development timer with saved recipes,
  agitation alerts and step timing.
- Lenses and bodies from your inventory fill in the numbers.

## Light Meter
- A reflected-light reading from the camera, averaged or from a spot you touch, or a reading you
  type in.
- Solves for aperture, shutter or ISO in third stops, with filter compensation and a calibration
  you can set against a meter you trust.
- Takes its ISO from a loaded film roll.

## Sun, Moon and Milky Way
- Golden and blue hours, twilight, sunrise and sunset with their directions on a map, and moon
  phases, for any place, in that place's time zone.
- Dark, moonless nights with the Milky Way's core up.
- Checked against the US Naval Observatory: sunrise, sunset and twilight within a minute.

## Shoots
- A log of planned and past shoots with their place, client, notes and that day's light, and a
  heads-up before golden hour.
- Kits and a packing check that flags gear that's lent out, due for service or due back.
- Shot lists from built-in or saved templates.
- A film roll log from loading to scanning, and how often each piece of gear gets used.

## Your data
- Syncs through your private iCloud database. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device.
- Export to JSON (the whole library, which imports back), CSV or PDF; automatic local backups;
  import that merges or replaces.

## Accessibility
- Dynamic Type, including the accessibility sizes; VoiceOver labels throughout, with chart
  moments moved by swiping up or down; dark mode.

## Known limits
- The light meter reads reflected light through the phone's camera, so it averages what the
  camera sees. Calibrate it against a meter or a test roll you trust.
- Only Nikon photos expose a shutter count that iOS can read.
- Reciprocity is given only for films whose maker publishes it. For the rest the app says so
  rather than guessing.
- Barcode lookups depend on UPCitemdb knowing the product; gear can always be entered by hand.
