Shutter Manager keeps your camera gear in order and puts the field tools photographers reach for in one place, on iPhone and iPad.

YOUR GEAR
• Bodies, lenses, lighting, filters, support, memory cards, drones and more, with photos, serial numbers, specs and values
• Fill in a camera's or lens's details from a photo it took, or scan a barcode
• Shutter counts charted against the rated life, sensor-cleaning reminders and a maintenance log
• Gear lent out or borrowed, with due dates and reminders
• Purchase price and current value, with searches on used-gear sites when it's time to sell

FIELD TOOLS
• Equivalent exposure, ND filters with a bulb timer, and Sunny 16
• Depth of field and hyperfocal distance, field of view and crop equivalents
• Astro exposure with the NPF and 500 rules, star trails, time-lapse, flash and print size
• Film reciprocity from the makers' own datasheets, and a development timer for your recipes
• Pick a lens or body from your gear and the tools fill in its numbers

LIGHT METER
• A reflected-light reading from the camera, averaged or from a spot you touch
• Solves for aperture, shutter or ISO in third stops, with filter compensation
• Meters for the film in a loaded roll

SUN, MOON AND MILKY WAY
• Golden and blue hours, twilight, and where the sun and moon rise and set, on a map
• Moon phases, and dark, moonless nights with the Milky Way's core up
• Any place: where you are, a saved place or a point on the map, in that place's own time zone

SHOOTS
• Plan a shoot with its place, client and that day's light, and get a heads-up before golden hour
• Pack with kits: gear that's lent out, due for service or due back is flagged
• Shot lists from built-in or saved templates, ticked off as you go
• A film roll log, and how often each piece of gear gets used

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud; the developer runs no servers
• Export to JSON, CSV or PDF, keep backups on your device, and restore when you need to

Every feature is free. If Shutter Manager helps you, there's a tip jar in Settings.
